Explore all screens and features

Marathon

Bring today’s focus into one plan

Choose 2, 3 or 4 Pomodoros of 25 or 50 minutes. Take a five-minute break between them, then confirm “I’m here” within one minute.

Finish the plan for a bonus. If you need to leave, completed sessions keep their regular points.

Explore Marathon

Mock exams

Prepare in your own exam rhythm

TYT, AYT, YDT, LGS Verbal/Quantitative, TUS Basic/Clinical, DUS and KPSS: choose an exam and solve without breaks after 3-2-1. KPSS includes General Ability–General Knowledge for Bachelor’s, Associate Degree and Secondary Education, plus nine separate Field Knowledge exams.

Enter or edit subject net scores later in the Mock exams section of Reports. When enough data is available, compare score changes with study time. Mock exam points and leagues stay separate from the normal league.

Everything you need to keep going

Timer and Pomodoro

Focus for 25 or 50 minutes. Complete sessions to earn points and sand and keep your streak.

Focus with friends

Add friends with friend codes and see who is focusing, on a Marathon break or taking a mock exam.

Monthly leagues

Monthly leagues start on 1 December 2026. Find your place within your goal and globally; normal and mock exam leagues are separate. Periods reset at 00:00 on the first of each month, Istanbul time.

Reports

See your daily, weekly and monthly totals. Record subject net scores and compare changes with study time when enough data is available.

Get started in three steps

1. Create an account

Sign up with email or Google. Apple sign-in is being prepared for iPhone.

2. Choose your goal

Choose Turkish or English and a goal: LGS, YKS, TUS, DUS, KPSS, language, university or work. Organise subjects and your daily target.

3. Start focusing

Start a Pomodoro, plan a Marathon or choose a mock exam.
