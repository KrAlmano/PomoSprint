Explore every feature

Marathon

Plan your next focus session

Choose 2, 3 or 4 Pomodoros of 25 or 50 minutes. Take a five-minute break between them, then confirm “I’m here” within one minute to continue.

  • Finish the full plan for a bonus.
  • If you need to leave, completed sessions keep their regular points.
  • Starting a Marathon and confirming after a break require internet.

Everything you need to keep going

Pomodoro and Marathon

Mock exams

Choose TYT, AYT, YDT, LGS or TUS, then follow an uninterrupted exam timer.

Monthly leagues
