PomoSprint: Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about Pomodoro, Marathon, LGS/YKS/TUS/DUS/KPSS mock exams and your progress.

Choose 25 or 50 minutes and start after a 3-2-1 countdown. One pause is allowed and reduces that session’s points by 20%. Marathon combines 2, 3 or 4 sessions with five-minute breaks. Confirm “I’m here” within one minute after each break. If you leave, completed sessions keep their regular points.

Elapsed time is preserved. An offline Pomodoro is saved on your phone and sent when connected, within 24 hours of starting. Starting a Marathon, confirming after a break and starting a new mock exam require internet. A running mock exam keeps the correct time even if the app closes.

Choose LGS, YKS, TUS, DUS, KPSS, language, university or work. You can plan TUS, DUS and KPSS preparation with the relevant subjects. Manage up to eight active study categories and change their names, colours and order.

Pomodoro and mock exam leagues are separate. Compete with people sharing your study goal in rooms of up to 25, and see all goals in the global ranking. Monthly leagues start on 1 December 2026 and reset at 00:00 on the first day of the month, Istanbul time. Study days and streaks still change at 04:00. A mid-month goal change applies next month without losing points. Preparation, promotion and award conditions are explained in the app.

No. Choose TYT, AYT, YDT, LGS Verbal, LGS Quantitative, TUS Basic, TUS Clinical, DUS or KPSS. Question count and duration are provided. After the 3-2-1 countdown, solve without pauses or breaks. The two LGS sessions are separate mock exams. KPSS General Ability–General Knowledge for Bachelor’s, Associate Degree and Secondary Education has 120 questions (60 + 60) and lasts 130 minutes. Each of the nine separate Field Knowledge exams has 40 questions; Statistics lasts 60 minutes and the other eight fields last 50 minutes. When you finish AYT before time runs out, choose “I’ve finished the exam” to record your actual study time. An abandoned attempt remains marked as abandoned in My exams and earns no points, sand or study time.

No. Correct and wrong answers calculate unanswered counts automatically, and you can still edit unanswered counts. Save only the subjects you have entered and fill in or edit the others later. Negative values and totals above the question count are rejected. Net scores use correct − wrong/4 for YKS, TUS, DUS and KPSS, and correct − wrong/3 for LGS; the English interface uses decimal dots. Scores are personal records and never change points, sand or leagues. You can add a name and note to an attempt. For AYT, first choose one or more of the four tests you completed, then enter results for their subjects. The server verifies rewards; entering net scores earns no rewards. Full-duration rewards are 60 sand for LGS Verbal, 70 for LGS Quantitative, 95 for YDT, 110 each for TUS Basic and Clinical, 120 for DUS, 130 for TYT and 145 for AYT. KPSS GY–GK earns 105 sand and 26 points; a 50-minute Field Knowledge exam earns 40 sand and 10 points; 60-minute Statistics earns 50 sand and 12 points. Early AYT rewards are proportional to actual time: 90 minutes earns 72 sand and 18 points.

When there are enough comparable attempts and study records, insights show changes in subject net scores alongside time spent studying. Missing data is not invented and correlation is not presented as proof of cause. Completed mock exams count toward total study time but are not divided among subjects.

Connect by sending and accepting friend-code requests. Friends can see “Focusing”, “On a break” or “Taking a mock exam”. Break status is only used for Marathon breaks. Visibility and blocking choices are respected. Your subject net scores are private.

There are 42 available characters: 7 starters, 4 unlocked for 200 sand each, 21 special avatars for 500 sand each, and 10 earned league awards. League awards cannot be bought. New mock exam league artwork will appear separately when ready.
