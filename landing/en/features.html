Features

PomoSprint: study time and progress together

How does your study time relate to your exam results? Follow your Pomodoros, Marathons and mock exams, and keep going with friends.

Focus

Timer and Pomodoro

Choose a subject and 25 or 50 minutes. After 3-2-1, the hourglass starts. Elapsed time stays correct when the app is closed or the screen is locked.

  • 25 minutes earns 5 points; 50 minutes earns 12. Each rewarded session earns 20 grains of sand.
  • One pause is allowed; using it reduces session points by 20%.
  • Notifications are optional. The study day and daily streak change at 04:00.
Marathon

Plan a short series of Pomodoros

Choose 2, 3 or 4 sessions of 25 or 50 minutes. Five-minute breaks separate your sessions.

  • At the end of each break, confirm “I’m here” within one minute. Otherwise the plan ends.
  • Finished sessions keep their regular points if you leave.
  • Full-plan bonuses: +2/+3/+5 for 25-minute sessions; +4/+6/+10 for 50-minute sessions.
  • Starting a plan and confirming after a break require internet.
Mock exams

Keep the rhythm of your exam

Select TYT, AYT, YDT, LGS Verbal/Numerical, or TUS Basic/Clinical. The question count and duration are provided for that exam. LGS sessions are offered separately.

The timer starts after 3-2-1. No pauses or breaks are available. Starting requires internet; elapsed time is preserved if the app closes.

  • An abandoned attempt remains in your history but earns no points or sand.
  • In My mock exams, see the date, start and end time, duration and result status.
  • Enter correct, incorrect and blank answers by subject. Edit them later if needed.
  • Net scores are personal records; they never change league points or sand.
Time and net scores

How does your study time relate to your exam results?

See changes in your subject net scores alongside the time you spent studying that subject. Insights require comparable attempts and enough data; a single result is not treated as proof of cause and effect.

Your total study time includes Pomodoro, Marathon and completed mock exams. Mock exam time remains a separate “Mock exam” category and is not assigned to individual subjects.

Reports

See today’s effort

See total study time, your daily goal and time by subject. Net scores and mock attempts have their own report.

  • Review daily, weekly and monthly totals.
  • Follow your study calendar and compare periods without counting sessions twice.
Weekly study

Your week at a glance

Review Monday through Sunday, your daily time and the subjects you focused on.

Monthly study

Build a habit day by day

A study calendar shows the days you worked and your accumulated effort. Compare this month with the previous one.

Together

Focus with your friends

Share your friend code or send a request using a friend’s code. Requests can be accepted or declined.

  • See “Focusing”, “On a break” and “Taking a mock exam”.
  • “On a break” is only used for Marathon breaks.
  • Choose whether to appear in the live list and public rankings from your profile. Blocking and privacy preferences are respected.
Leagues

A shared goal, a little friendly competition

Compete in a monthly league with people who share your study goal, and see your place in the global ranking.

  1. Bronze League
  2. Silver League
  3. Gold League
  4. Sapphire League
  5. Diamond League
Detailed rules

Pomodoro and mock exam leagues are separate. LGS, YKS, TUS and other study goals have their own groups; global rankings bring all goals together. Rooms hold up to 25 people. A preparation state is shown until there is enough participation.

Both leagues reset at 00:00 on the first day of each month, Istanbul time. Study days and daily streaks continue to change at 04:00. If you change your goal mid-month, you keep your points; the new goal group applies next month.

Promotion and awards require the period’s activity conditions. The app explains the current rules. Bronze League has no relegation.

Only server-verified, full-duration mock exams earn separate mock exam points and sand. Entered net scores never affect rewards or rankings.

Make it your study space

Offline focus

A single Pomodoro can finish offline. It is saved on your phone and sent when connected, within 24 hours of starting. Marathon confirmations and new mock exams need internet.

Subjects and goals

Choose LGS, YKS, TUS, KPSS, language, university or work. Manage up to eight active study categories and set a daily goal.

Language and theme

Choose Turkish or English during onboarding and change it later. The app supports light and dark themes, plus a simple landscape focus view.

Notifications

Choose session-end notifications and a lock-screen timer. Availability depends on device permissions and platform support.

Your account

Change your display name and avatar, reset your password, or delete your account from the profile screen.

Your privacy

Control public visibility, manage friend requests, and block or report unwanted users. Net scores and insights remain personal.

Collection

Turn your sand into characters

Discover 42 available characters: 7 starters, 4 unlocked for 200 sand each, 21 special avatars for 500 sand each, and 10 earned league awards. League awards cannot be bought. Mock exam league awards have a separate collection area; new artwork will be added when ready.
