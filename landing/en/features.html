PomoSprint: study time and progress together

How does your study time relate to your exam results? Follow your Pomodoros, Marathons and mock exams, and keep going with friends.

Focus

Timer and Pomodoro

Choose a category and 25 or 50 minutes. After 3-2-1, focus with the hourglass. Elapsed time stays correct when the screen is locked or the app is closed.

Take your own break after a single Pomodoro. Session-end notifications depend on your permissions and preference.

  • 25 minutes earns 5 base points; 50 minutes earns 12; each rewarded session earns 20 grains of sand
  • One pause is allowed; using it reduces base session points by 20%
  • Study days and daily streaks change at 04:00 in your selected timezone
Marathon

Bring today’s focus into one plan

Choose 2, 3 or 4 Pomodoros of 25 or 50 minutes. Progress through completed sessions and take a five-minute break between them.

When a break ends, confirm “I’m here” within one minute. Starting and confirming after a break require internet. If you cannot continue, completed sessions keep their regular points.

  • A completed plan of 2/3/4 × 25-minute sessions earns +2/+3/+5 bonus points
  • A completed plan of 2/3/4 × 50-minute sessions earns +4/+6/+10 bonus points
Mock exams

Keep your exam rhythm

Choose TYT, AYT, YDT, LGS Verbal, LGS Quantitative, TUS Basic, TUS Clinical, DUS or KPSS. Question count and duration are provided. The two LGS sessions are separate mock exams.

After the 3-2-1 countdown, solve without pauses or breaks. Internet is required to start; elapsed time remains correct when the screen is locked or the app is closed.

When you have finished AYT, choose “I’ve finished the exam” before time runs out. Your actual study time is recorded; this is separate from abandoning an attempt.

KPSS sessions and rewards

General Ability–General Knowledge (GY–GK) is a single 130-minute session with 120 questions (60 + 60) for Bachelor’s, Associate Degree and Secondary Education.

Field Knowledge includes nine separate exams with 40 questions each: Business Administration, Accounting, Law, Public Administration, Economics, Public Finance, International Relations, Labour Economics and Industrial Relations, and Statistics. Statistics lasts 60 minutes; each of the other eight fields lasts 50 minutes.

My exams

Record your result and keep improving

Find your completed attempts, dates and status in the Mock exams section of Reports. Enter correct and wrong answers to calculate unanswered questions automatically, or edit unanswered counts yourself.

Add a name and note to your attempt. For AYT, first choose the tests you completed: Turkish Language and Literature–Social Sciences-1, Social Sciences-2, Mathematics and Science. Each test has 40 questions; enter correct and wrong counts for the subjects within it.

Save only the subjects you have entered and finish the others later. Negative values and totals above the question count are rejected. Offline entries are kept on your device with a clear sending status.

  • YKS, TUS, DUS and KPSS: correct − wrong/4; LGS: correct − wrong/3
  • Decimal net scores use commas in Turkish and dots in English
  • Entering or editing net scores never changes points, sand or rankings
Time and net scores

See study time alongside changes in net scores

When there are enough comparable attempts in the last three weeks, compare a subject’s net score changes with time spent in related study categories.

Your scores and insights are personal. Missing data is not invented, and correlation is not proof of cause. Mock exam time is never divided among subjects.

Daily study

See today’s effort in one total

Pomodoro, Marathon and completed mock exams all contribute to study time. Track your daily goal, session-type counts, category minutes and percentages together.

Mock exam time has its own row. Abandoned attempts do not count toward study time or completed exam totals.

  • Adjust your daily goal from the same screen
  • Study days and streaks change at 04:00 in your selected timezone
A wider view

Follow your week and month

See study time by day in the weekly chart and your routine in the monthly calendar. Review totals across Pomodoro, Marathon and completed mock exams.

Mock exam time stays separate in category distribution, so time is never counted twice. Report study days change at 04:00; monthly league periods change at 00:00 Istanbul time.

Monthly leagues

Compete within your goal, find your global place

LGS, YKS, TUS, DUS, KPSS and other goals compete in their own categories. Your category room holds up to 25 people; the global ranking brings all goals together.

Monthly leagues start on 1 December 2026 and reset at 00:00 on the first day of each month, Istanbul time. A mid-month goal change keeps your points and applies to your new group next month.

  • The normal league uses Pomodoro and Marathon points only
  • A preparation state appears until there are enough active participants
  • Current activity requirements for promotion and awards are explained in the app

Detailed league rules

A room stays in preparation until enough active people participate. Promotion and league awards require the period’s activity conditions; see the app’s information button for current rules. Bronze League has no relegation.

A separate competition

A league for your mock exam effort

Completed mock exams verified by the server earn mock exam league points and separate exam sand. They do not affect normal league points or normal sand.

The mock exam league also has study-goal and global views, with a new period at 00:00 on the first of each month, Istanbul time. Entered net scores never affect rankings.

Study together

See what your friends are doing now

Send and accept requests with friend codes. See friends as “Focusing”, “On a break” during Marathon breaks, or “Taking a mock exam”.

Manage live-list visibility from your profile. Blocking and visibility choices are respected; subject scores and personal insights are not shared.

Make it your study space

Offline focus

A single Pomodoro can finish offline. It is saved on your phone and sent when connected, within 24 hours of starting. Marathon confirmations and new mock exams need internet.

Subjects and goals

Choose LGS, YKS, TUS, DUS, KPSS, language, university or work. Manage up to eight active study categories and set a daily goal.

Language and theme

Choose Turkish or English during onboarding and change it later. The app supports light and dark themes, plus a simple landscape focus view.

Notifications

Choose session-end notifications and a lock-screen timer. Availability depends on device permissions and platform support.

Your account

Change your display name and avatar, reset your password, or delete your account from the profile screen.

Your privacy

Control public visibility, manage friend requests, and block or report unwanted users. Net scores and insights remain personal.

Collection

Turn your sand into characters

Discover 42 available characters: 7 starters, 4 unlocked for 200 sand each, 21 special avatars for 500 sand each, and 10 earned league awards. League awards cannot be bought. Mock exam league award artwork will appear separately when ready.
